Important Information

Indexed universal life (IUL) and other cash-value life insurance is life insurance. It is not an investment, a bank account or a security, it is not FDIC insured, and it is not right for everyone.

Costs and risks

  • Policies have fees and charges, highest in the early years. Surrender charges apply for a number of years, so you can get back less than you paid in if you cancel early.
  • Indexed interest credits are subject to caps and participation rates the insurance company can change, and may be zero. The 0% floor applies to index credits only; fees can still reduce cash value.
  • A policy may need more premium than planned to stay in force. Coverage requires medical and financial underwriting.

Taxes and policy loans

  • Cash value grows tax-deferred, and the death benefit is generally income-tax free.
  • Policy loans and withdrawals accrue interest and reduce cash value and death benefit. They may be taxable if the policy lapses, is surrendered or is a modified endowment contract (MEC).
  • We do not give tax or legal advice. Talk with your own tax and legal advisors.

Illustrations and guarantees

  • Illustrations and examples are hypothetical and not guaranteed. Always review the guaranteed values.
  • Guarantees are based on the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company.
  • Replacing an existing policy may not be in your best interest; it can mean new surrender charges, costs, underwriting and a new contestability period.
