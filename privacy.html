Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Last updated October 20, 2026

Unshakeable Financial Partners LLC ("we," "us") is an independent insurance agency. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, forms, phone line and text messages, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, state, the topics you're interested in, and anything you write in a form or tell us on a call.
  • Consent records: whether you agreed to be contacted by call or text, when, on which page, and the wording you agreed to.
  • Call and message records: calls to and from our business line may be recorded and transcribed for quality and compliance. Texts and emails are logged in our customer records system.
  • Website data: standard analytics such as pages visited, device and browser type, and how you arrived at our site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and schedule appointments with a licensed agent.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders and follow-up information you asked for.
  • To keep records required by insurance and telemarketing regulations.
  • To improve our website and services.

If you request a quote or apply for coverage, we share the information needed with the insurance companies we work with, as part of that process.

Text messages (SMS)

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or your text-message opt-in consent with any third party or affiliate for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties, except the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf.

  • Program: Unshakeable Financial Partners appointment, reminder and follow-up messages, sent from (435) 466-4302.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You'll receive one message confirming you've been unsubscribed.
  • Help: reply HELP, call (435) 232-3234, or email the address below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Agreeing to receive texts is never a condition of any purchase.

Calls, including AI-assisted calls

If you gave us permission on our website form, we may call you at the number you provided, including with an AI assistant that identifies itself as an AI at the start of the call. Calls may be recorded. You can ask us to stop calling at any time, by phone, text or email, and we will honor it promptly.

Cookies and advertising tools

Our website uses cookies and similar tools, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to measure visits and to show our ads to people who have visited our site. These tools may record which pages you view and whether you click a booking button, and they may set a cookie that lasts up to 180 days. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not send your name, phone number, email or any health or financial details you share with us to advertising platforms.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.

How we protect your information

We use reputable service providers with access controls and encryption in transit. We keep records only as long as needed for our business and as required by law.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Stop texts by replying STOP, and stop calls by telling us.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us below, subject to records we're legally required to keep.
