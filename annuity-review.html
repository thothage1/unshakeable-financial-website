Important Information

Annuities are insurance contracts issued by insurance companies. They are not bank deposits, are not FDIC or NCUA insured, are not obligations of or guaranteed by any bank, and are not right for everyone.

Costs and access to your money

  • Most annuities have surrender charges, and some have market value adjustments, if you withdraw more than the free-withdrawal amount during the surrender period. You could get back less than you put in if you surrender early.
  • Optional riders, such as lifetime income riders, may carry annual fees.
  • Cashing in a CD before it matures may cost an early-withdrawal penalty.

Taxes

  • Annuity earnings grow tax-deferred. Withdrawals of earnings are taxed as ordinary income, and withdrawals before age 59½ may be subject to a 10% federal additional tax.
  • Rollovers from retirement plans have their own rules. We do not give tax or legal advice; talk with your own tax and legal advisors.

Guarantees and comparisons

  • Guarantees are based on the financial strength and claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company.
  • Fixed indexed annuities are not invested in the stock market; credited interest is subject to caps, participation rates or spreads the company can change, and may be zero.
  • Rates change frequently and vary by product, term, amount and state. Replacing an existing annuity may not be in your best interest: it can start a new surrender period and add costs. Recommendations follow Utah's best-interest rule for annuity sales (Utah Admin. Code R590-230).
