What we'll cover

  • Protect: key-person coverage, and how it keeps the business steady if someone important is gone
  • Transfer: buy-sell agreements, the ways to fund them, and what a 2024 Supreme Court case means for many of them
  • Keep: ways owners reward and hold on to their best people

Who it's for

Owners, partners and founders of businesses with roughly 5 to 100 employees who want to understand their options before they need them.
